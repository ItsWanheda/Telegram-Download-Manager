# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate
* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on technical issues rather than individuals
* Respecting different viewpoints
* Helping new contributors
* Giving credit where appropriate

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Trolling
* Deliberate intimidation
* Threats
* Discrimination
* Sexualized language or unwanted advances
* Publishing private information without permission
* Deliberate disruption of project discussions
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project.

Examples include:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Project documentation
* Community communication channels
* Project-related events

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers.

Reports should include:

* Description of the incident
* Relevant links
* Screenshots or evidence where appropriate
* Any additional context that may help with investigation

Maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate action.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may take actions including:

1. Warning the contributor
2. Removing inappropriate content
3. Temporarily restricting participation
4. Permanently restricting participation

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the incident.
